### Family that beats α

Source checkpoint: `best_families/BELOW_caps_1003.json` (mode `caps`, seed 1003, restart 8).

**Construction** (broken-rung ladder):

- Cap length L=100, period-10 Chen core residues {3,6,9} on the middle broken set.
- Fixed **asymmetric** caps (see JSON / `below_limit_certify.py`): `left_cap` and `right_cap` differ from each other and from the certified CAP.
- Sizes n = 248+20m (m≥0), same caps, growing Chen core.

At every computed size the maximizing incomparable pair is the **top** endpoint (p,q)=(n/2,n/2), and δ equals that pair’s minority probability.

**Exact limit** (transfer-matrix / spectral, same 6-state band as Chen; charpoly of period blocks A,B = z⁴(z²−164z+27)):

```

    / 202386202979838556018601359840018770821908
  ≈ 0.34884345959285371889017174724320154670
```

**Comparison:** α − β ≈ 5.665080252714171×10⁻¹³ > 0, so **β < α**.

Verification: `logs/below_asymptotics.json` (n=248…428), `logs/below_limit_exact.json`, `below_limit_certify.py` (matches exact DP minority/E for m=0..3 and simplifies lim−β=0).

Thus α is **not** the exact infimum over broken-rung ladders: an explicit algebraic improvement exists.

### Spectral notes (`PROOF_ATTEMPT.md`)

- Fixed-core lemma: with Chen transfer matrix A (A³−164A²+27A=0), far asymmetric caps cannot improve a **fixed** left core’s left-endpoint limit; changing the cap/core can.
- Endpoint-only inequalities cannot prove a lower bound (Catalan n=8 example has endpoint 1/3 but δ>1/3 by Sah).
- Six-state band is tied to intact-rung placement; not automatic for all broken-rung ladders.

### Sah 2021 (arXiv:1811.01500) reduction sketch

Sah proves the width-2 case of the 1/3–2/3 conjecture and studies balance via lattice paths / recursive structure. A reduction from general width-2 posets to broken-rung ladders would need roughly:

1. Every width-2 poset is two chains with cross relations; encode as monotone boundary maps (f,g) as here.
2. Show that near-extremizers can be replaced by ladders whose cross relations are generated by i≺i+2 and a subset of i≺i+3 (rung pattern), without raising δ — likely needing a local rewriting / uncrossing argument.
3. Show caps can be truncated or periodized so that a spectral limit is controlled by a finite transfer matrix.
4. Only then does a ladder-class lower bound lift to γ₂.

No such rewriting is claimed here; Sah’s methods are the natural template but the gap is substantial.
